# Telegraph Protocol Hackathon – Season I H1

**Official site**: https://hackathon.telegraphprotocol.com/  
**Submissions close**: 7 Sep 2026, 12:00 UTC  
**Current window (as of 23 Aug 2026)**: Track 1 (Miners) & Track 2 (Scripts) open until ~31 Aug; Track 3 (Apps/Agents) 31 Aug – 7 Sep.

## Prize Pool (H1)
$5,000 USD total (part of $15k Season I series).

Approximate distribution (from official materials):
- Miner Track: 1st $2,000 · 2nd $1,000 · 3rd $600 · 4th $400
- Script Author Track: 1st $1,000 · 2nd $500 · 3rd $300 · 4th $200
- Application Track: 1st $2,000 · 2nd $1,000 · 3rd $600 · 4th $400

## Tracks (judged independently)
1. **Miner Track** – Wrap any API/model/dataset/tool via YAML.     Judging: Telegraph ranking & performance + apps built on it + total requests served + X progress posts & engagement.
2. **Script Author Track** – Write evaluation scripts (WASM) that score/rank miners.     Judging: Automated eval of script + ranking accuracy + resistance to gaming + X posts & engagement.
3. **Application Track** – Build real products/agents/workflows that consume live Telegraph Miners.     Judging: Users & activity + usage/adoption + creativity + must use real Miners + X engagement.

## Key Rules & Guardrails
- Tracks 1 & 2 must remain live during Track 3.
- Applications must use real live Miners (no mocks/simulations).
- Intent must have ≥3 active Miners and ≥100 real Track 3 requests to be eligible for global cash prizes.
- All progress updates for judging must be public X posts properly tagging @Telegraphprotoc.
- Artificial inflation / gaming = disqualification.
- Must join official Hackathon Discord.
- One clear intent per YAML preferred.
- Min price 0.01 USDC.

## Timeline
- Track 1 & 2: 17 Aug – 31 Aug 2026
- Track 3: 31 Aug – 7 Sep 2026
- Winner selection & announcement: after 7 Sep

## Core Thesis Being Tested
Ranked + verified + economically incentivized intelligence layer that gets stronger with real usage (quality flywheel).
